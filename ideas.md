# Cosmic Platform — Experience Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Astral Index  
**Very Brief Intro:** A contemporary esoteric editorial system: the warmth and tactility of a well-used almanac combined with the precision of a celestial research interface. It feels cultivated, private, and intellectually curious rather than mystical-by-default.  
**Probability:** 0.07

### Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Lunar House  
**Very Brief Intro:** A soft, domestic ritual experience built from moonlit neutrals, paper textures, and intimate journal fragments. The product would feel calming and gentle, with a slower emotional tempo.  
**Probability:** 0.04

### Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Signal from Elsewhere  
**Very Brief Intro:** A bold, digital-horoscope universe defined by hard black, alert color, oversized numerals, and kinetic status markers. It is modern, provocative, and engineered for high-energy cultural relevance.  
**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Astral Index

### Design Movement
**Contemporary esoteric editorialism** informed by independent print journals, scientific field notes, celestial cartography, and Bauhaus-era information design. The site should make the intangible feel legible: structured readings, transparent signals, and layered personal interpretation.

### Core Principles
1. **Pattern over prediction.** Every surface frames insight as a discernible personal pattern, not a deterministic forecast.
2. **Editorial hierarchy.** Reading experiences alternate between oversized human language and small, factual source labels.
3. **Quiet complexity.** Dense planetary, numeric, and bodygraph signals are organized as composed layers with generous breathing room.
4. **Tactile contrast.** Parchment-white reading fields are interrupted by high-ink navigation and dark constellation panels to distinguish reflective content from real-time signals.

### Color Philosophy
The palette pairs **warm paper** and a very deep blue-black ink to create an almanac-like authority, without the generic violet gradient of mainstream astrology products. Small hits of comet vermilion act like a red editorial annotation: an ownable, kinetic indicator of what is active right now. Mineral blue grounds the system diagrams and communicates calculation transparency.

### Layout Paradigm
The experience uses a **reading-desk composition**, not a centered SaaS template. A slim vertical index and deep left-hand context lead into an asymmetrical right-hand content field. Hero copy, index marks, and profile diagnostics deliberately sit on different baselines, as if the user is moving through a living research dossier.

### Signature Elements
1. **Orbital traces:** thin interrupted circular paths and coordinate ticks that connect cards and chart areas.
2. **Source tags:** compact monospace system identifiers such as `ASTRO / HD / NUM` that explain where an insight originates.
3. **Comet marks:** a small vermilion half-disc or dot used for live activations, current-day emphasis, and calls to action.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should make analysis feel inspectable. Hovering cards reveals their source signals, tabs shift the reading’s lens, and buttons respond with an immediate, modest physical press. Navigation is direct and calm; any future unavailable product paths state that they are in development rather than pretending to work.

### Animation
Motion is restrained and data-like. Use opacity and 8–16px transforms for content entrances, with a stagger no longer than 80ms. Orbital lines may slowly rotate only where they remain decorative, and the live comet mark may softly pulse. Every transition uses a decisive ease-out under 280ms; motion is removed for people with reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** is reserved for expressive reading headlines and large diagnostic numbers, giving the product literary gravity. **Manrope** handles UI, navigation, and body text with clean clarity. **IBM Plex Mono** is used sparingly for source tags, dates, formulas, coordinates, and system status. The interface should contrast expressive serif at large scale with disciplined sans-serif for operation.

### Brand Essence
**Cosmic is a transparent self-pattern engine for people who want their astrology, numerology, and Human Design to read as one useful daily language.**  
**Personality:** precise, contemplative, magnetic.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, quietly evocative, and concrete; CTAs are verbs that invite examination instead of hype. Microcopy explains the provenance of every reading.

> “Your pattern is active. Read the signal, not the noise.”

> “Open today’s brief — three systems, one coherent lens.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo is an **open orbital monogram**: a bold, asymmetric `C` suggested by a partial eclipse and a single orbiting dot. It is symbol-only in the interface, paired with a custom spaced COSMIC wordmark in navigation.

### Signature Brand Color
**Comet Vermilion — #EF5D3F**. It appears only where the experience needs to signal present-tense energy, active states, or a personal invitation.
